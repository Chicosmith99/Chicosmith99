✨ What Is It?

This is a custom tool built specially for COJIM's official Facebook and YouTube pages. It works like a digital security guard, always watching and protecting the ministry from:

* Fake comments
* Scams asking for donations
* People pretending to represent the church
* Evil or misleading messages


 🎯 Why Was It Built?

The internet is filled with bad actors. Some try to **trick the church’s followers, especially during live videos or under powerful sermons.
This extension ensures that God’s work remains pure online**, just like it is in person.


 🔍 What Does It Do?

**The extension does 3 big things automatically:**

1. **Watches YouTube and Facebook every second**, even during livestreams.
2. **Detects bad or suspicious comments**, including:

   * People asking for money
   * Strange links or WhatsApp numbers
   * Religious manipulation or spiritual confusion
3. **Takes action when danger is found:**

   * Saves the comment privately
   * Alerts the admin
   * Logs the time and reason

We built a secure online dashboard where you (or someone trusted) can:

* View all flagged comments
* Add or remove trusted words and websites
* Add new admin emails
* Review custom spam rules
